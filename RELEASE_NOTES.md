# X1 button alignment

Version 0.3.1 focuses on the Attack Shark X1 button view.

- Traced left/right button outlines around the physical edges and wheel housing.
- Dedicated wheel and side-button hit areas; highlights stay inside their own outlines.
- The image and clickable paths scale together, including the side view.
- Keyboard selection is preserved. A browser regression page checks geometry, seams and hit targets at three sizes.

G305 and Model O layouts, device commands, DPI control, profiles and saved preferences are unchanged. This is a visual alignment patch, not new primary-button hardware remapping support.

Download **UnnamedEnhancements.exe** below and open it directly. No ZIP extraction is needed for this release asset.
